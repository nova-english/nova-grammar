LEVEL 3 · Chapter 11 · Unit 3

화법 전환

일치와 화법 · 본책 p.197~ · Nova Grammar 예문 듣기

번호를 듣고 문장을 따라 읽어 보세요. 처음엔 글을 보며, 두 번째는 글을 가리고 들어 보세요.

  1. 1389He said, “I am tired.”→ He said (that) he was tired.
    그는 피곤하다고 말했다.
  2. 1390She said to me, “I like your bag.”→ She told me (that) she liked my bag.
    그녀는 내 가방이 마음에 든다고 말했다.
  3. 1391Jun said, “I will visit you tomorrow.”→ Jun said (that) he would visit me the next day.
    준은 다음 날 나를 찾아오겠다고 말했다.
  4. 1392She said to me, “Are you hungry?”→ She asked me if I was hungry.
    그녀는 나에게 배가 고픈지 물었다.
  5. 1393Mom said to me, “Where are you going?”→ Mom asked me where I was going.
    엄마는 나에게 어디에 가는지 물으셨다.
  6. 1394The teacher said, “Who broke the window?”→ The teacher asked who had broken the window.
    선생님께서 누가 창문을 깼는지 물으셨다. (의문사가 주어 → 바로 동사)
  7. 1395He said to me, “Did you see my cat?”→ He asked me if I had seen his cat.
    그는 나에게 자기 고양이를 보았는지 물었다. (did see → had seen)
  8. 1396She said to me, “How old are you?”→ She asked me how old I was.
    그녀는 나에게 몇 살인지 물었다. (how old는 한 덩어리)
  9. 1397Mom said to me, “Clean your room.”→ Mom told me to clean my room.
    엄마는 나에게 방을 청소하라고 말씀하셨다.
  10. 1398She said to me, “Please help me.”→ She asked me to help her.
    그녀는 나에게 도와 달라고 부탁했다.
  11. 1399The doctor said to him, “Get some rest.”→ The doctor advised him to get some rest.
    의사는 그에게 좀 쉬라고 충고했다.
  12. 1400Dad said to me, “Don’t be late.”→ Dad told me not to be late.
    아빠는 나에게 늦지 말라고 말씀하셨다.
